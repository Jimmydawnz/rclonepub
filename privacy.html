Privacy Policy

This application is for personal use. Google Drive authorization allows rclone to upload and manage my training files in my own Google Drive account.

Uploaded files are stored in my Google Drive. Authorization credentials are stored locally in rclone's configuration and used to authenticate transfers. This application does not sell Google user data or share it with third parties.

I can revoke the application's access through my Google Account settings and delete uploaded files directly from Google Drive.
